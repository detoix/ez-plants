# Thuja foliage plate

`leaf.webp` is an alpha-matted derivative of
[`2020 year. Herbarium. Thuja. img-002.jpg`](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2020_year._Herbarium._Thuja._img-002.jpg),
a scanograph photographed by Dmitry Makeev on 27 September 2020.

The source photograph and this derivative are licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Changes made for this project: one connected terminal scale spray was cropped
from the herbarium scan and rotated upright; the adjacent branch, neutral paper
background, and scan shadow were alpha-matted; residual white spill was removed
from partially transparent edge pixels; the natural foliage received a
restrained brightness and saturation correction; and the result was fitted to a
1024 x 1024 transparent plate and lossy WebP-compressed. No foliage pixels were
painted, synthesized, or replaced.
