# Echinacea leaf plate

`leaf.webp` is derived from “Starr 080117-1580 Echinacea purpurea” by Forest &
Kim Starr, downloaded from Wikimedia Commons:

https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Starr_080117-1580_Echinacea_purpurea.jpg

The source photograph is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0
Unported:

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Changes: one leaf was extracted from the photograph, reoriented onto a
transparent square plate, resized to 1024 × 1024 pixels and compressed as WebP.
