EZ-Plants garden twins

A growing library of plants for Polish and European gardens. Each plant models persistent cane structure, age, phenology and care events. Dates and care cues are educational scenario inputs—not a diagnosis or yield forecast.

Blackcurrant ‘Tisel’ uses the mean 2022–2024 IO-PIB observations for flowering, colouring and maturity. Forsythia ‘Lynwood’ uses the RHS size and pruning envelope with the central-Poland turn-of-March/April flowering peak, and flowers on bare one- and two-year-old wood before any leaf opens. Hydrangea paniculata ‘Limelight’ follows measured RHS and Chicago trial dimensions, flowers on current-season terminal shoots from July into October, and retains dry panicles through winter. Echinacea purpurea ‘Magnus’ follows its documented upright habit, large horizontal rose-purple rays and July–September Polish display, then retains selected prickly seed heads through winter. Thuja occidentalis ‘Smaragd’ follows the narrow, evergreen cultivar habit and overlapping flattened scale sprays, with a restrained spring flush and no invented winter bronzing. Visual phase durations are explicitly modeled assumptions; weather can shift a real plant by weeks.

The procedural renderer is built on EZ-Tree, created by Dan Greenheck and distributed under the MIT License.
