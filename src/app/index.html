Garden digital twin · research prototype

Tisel blackcurrant

This proof explores persistent cane structure, age, phenology, pruning and harvest events. Dates and care cues are educational scenario inputs—not a diagnosis or yield forecast.

The default season uses the mean 2022–2024 IO-PIB observations for flowering onset, berry colouring onset and full maturity. Visual phase durations are explicitly modeled assumptions; weather can shift a real plant by weeks.

The procedural renderer is built on EZ-Tree, created by Dan Greenheck and distributed under the MIT License.
